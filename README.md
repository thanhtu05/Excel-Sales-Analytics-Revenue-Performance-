# Excel Sales Analytics & Revenue Performance | VTI Electronics

**November 2026**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance and revenue trends for VTI Electronics using Microsoft Excel. The analysis consolidates sales order data across products, customers, regions, and sales staff to evaluate revenue distribution and identify business performance trends.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables & PivotCharts
- Data Cleaning
- Data Modeling
- KPI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Highlights

- Consolidated and cleaned **509 sales orders** across product, customer, region, and staff data to assess where revenue was generated and how sales varied over time.

- Analyzed **VND 13.88B** in reported revenue through monthly, product-category, and regional breakdowns; surfaced the peak sales month and leading revenue region for targeted performance reviews.

- Delivered an executive KPI dashboard tracking revenue, order volume, and sales mix; recommended reviewing underperforming periods and regions alongside top-performing product categories to inform sales planning.

## 🔍 Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Identify peak sales periods.
- Evaluate order volume and revenue fluctuations.

### 2. Product Category Analysis
- Compare revenue contributions across product categories.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Support product and inventory planning.

### 3. Regional Sales Analysis
- Evaluate revenue distribution across regions.
- Identify leading and underperforming regions.
- Support regional sales performance reviews.

### 4. Executive KPI Dashboard
- Total Revenue
- Total Sales Orders
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Product Category Sales Mix
- Regional Revenue Performance

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Review underperforming sales periods to identify potential causes of revenue fluctuations.
- Evaluate regional performance and identify opportunities to improve weaker markets.
- Prioritize top-performing product categories in sales and inventory planning.
- Monitor revenue, order volume, and sales mix regularly to support data-driven decisions.

- Apply Excel skills to real-world sales analytics scenarios.
